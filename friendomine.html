Friend'O'Mine

A private AI companion that lives on your phone. No cloud processing, no data leaving your device.

Join the Android beta All products

What you get

Private by design

Powered by IBM Granite models that run entirely on your phone. No cloud sync, no tracking and no ads, and it keeps working offline once your models are downloaded.

Conversations, your way

Chat in text with a fully on-device assistant. Your history stays with you, and you can export your data whenever you like.

Offline transcription

Turn speech into text without an internet connection. Get speaker-attributed transcripts of your recordings, all processed on your phone.

Hands-free voice mode

Talk naturally and listen to spoken replies, ideal when your hands or eyes are busy.

Helpful tools

Check the weather, work out dates and times, handle files and keep memory notes that carry across conversations.

Read-aloud

Hear responses spoken back, with voice, speed and volume tuned to your preference.

Multilingual

Converse in English, Spanish, German or Chinese.

Built for accessibility

Full TalkBack support, scalable text and high-contrast modes, so everyone can use it comfortably.

Encrypted storage

Everything saved on your device is protected with AES-256-GCM and hardware-backed keys from the Android Keystore.

Light and dark themes

A calm, comfortable look in both themes that follows your system setting.
