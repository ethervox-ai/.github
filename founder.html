Workspace Founder Programme

Join an exclusive group of 100 professionals helping shape the future of local-first, AI-assisted file management.

Pre-Release Programme

Ethervox Workspace is in active development. This is a pre-1.0 founder programme for professionals who want perpetual commercial licences and are willing to provide feedback on real-world usage with their own documents.

What You Get

  • Perpetual licence — works forever, no expiration
  • All updates forever — every future version, no additional cost
  • All platforms — macOS, iPadOS, Windows, Linux (as they launch)
  • Commercial use granted — use it professionally for your own work
  • Numbered licence — #001 through #100, yours to keep
  • Priority support — direct line to the development team
  • Preferential team pricing — if you later need Practice seats for your organization

The Grant Terms

Your founder licence grants you:

Perpetual, non-transferable, single-user licence for all updates, all platforms in the Workspace line, with commercial use by one named person.

What this means:

  • Perpetual: No expiration date. Ever.
  • All updates: Every build we release, on all platforms, forever.
  • Commercial use: You can use Workspace professionally for your own work — law practice, medical practice, consulting, research, whatever you do.
  • Single user: The licence is issued to you personally and covers your use across your own devices.
  • Non-transferable: It stays with you; it can't be sold or reassigned.

What It's Not

  • Not a site licence — it doesn't cover your colleagues or team
  • Not for shared machines — it's for your personal use
  • Not an organizational deployment — if you need Workspace for your practice/firm/team, contact us about Practice seats

Using Workspace at Your Organization?

If you're using your founder licence professionally and think your colleagues would benefit, we'd love to help. Founders get priority onboarding and preferential pricing for Practice seats.

This is a one-time offer, not a recurring prompt. Use your licence however works for you — we won't nag or gate anything.

The 100 Cap

Exactly 100 licences. Numbered #001–#100. Never reopened.

Why 100? It's large enough to test distribution and gather diverse real-world feedback, yet small enough that every founder is genuinely scarce and valued. We're not building a free tier disguised as exclusivity — we're building relationships with 100 professionals whose feedback will shape the product.

What We're Looking For

Professionals who:

  • Have messy, real-world document corpora (patient files, case files, scans, 20 years of notes)
  • Care about privacy and local-first software
  • Are willing to share feedback on what works and what doesn't
  • Understand this is pre-1.0 software and will have rough edges

You don't need to be technical. We're looking for real-world usage, not code contributions.

The Expectation

In exchange for a perpetual commercial licence, we ask (but don't enforce):

  • Try Workspace with your real documents
  • Share what works and what doesn't (via the built-in feedback tool)
  • Be patient with pre-1.0 limitations

This is a stated expectation, not a contract requirement. Your licence doesn't expire if you don't provide feedback. But if you do, you're directly shaping what Workspace becomes.

How to Join

Send an email to founder@ethervox.ai with:

  • Your name
  • Your profession or what you'd use Workspace for
  • Why you're interested in local-first, AI-assisted file management
  • Platform preference (macOS, iPadOS, Windows, Linux)

No payment, no credit card, no form to fill out. Just an email. We'll review applications and issue licences manually as the programme fills.

Timeline

  • Now: Accepting applications
  • Wave 1 (100 licences): Issued as macOS/iPadOS v1.0 approaches release readiness
  • After 100: Programme closed forever

Privacy & Data

Workspace processes everything locally on your device. We don't see your documents, ever. The only data we receive is what you choose to send via the feedback tool — and that's a local mail draft you review before sending, containing only app version, device tier, and anonymized counters if you choose to include them.

See our Privacy Policy for details.

Questions?

Email founder@ethervox.ai — we're here to help.
