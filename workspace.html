Ethervox Workspace

A local-first, AI-assisted file manager, viewer and editor. Browse, search, read and organise your documents with an assistant that runs entirely on your Mac.

Download for macOS (.dmg) Request Beta License

Version 0.4.2 · macOS 14 (Sonoma) or later · Apple Silicon

What you get

Private by design

AI models run locally. No cloud processing, no advertising or analytics SDKs, no usage metering.

Plan, preview, apply

The assistant never changes your files silently. Every change is a reviewable plan you approve first.

Undo and history

Every applied change is journalled and can be undone. Deleted items go to a trash with configurable retention.

Search and ask

Index a folder and search it, or ask questions about your documents in plain language.

Accessible

Built for VoiceOver, Dynamic Type and keyboard use. Accessibility features are never behind a paywall.

Your data is yours

Data export is always available and never gated.

Custom workflows

Save prompts and change requests that you run on a regular basis. Apply them to the whole workspace, folders, files or a selection.

Custom workspace rules

Give the assistant guidance on how to handle specific types of information in your workspace.

Transcription Coming soon

Turn audio into text, entirely on your Mac.
