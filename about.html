About EthervoxAI

Artificial intelligence that works for you

Our Mission

Democratize AI while preserving privacy. Everyone should have access to powerful AI without trading away their personal data. We build tools that run on the devices people already own, so your information stays with you.

What We Stand For

Privacy by design

Privacy is part of every decision from the first line of code. We collect no analytics, show no ads and run no tracking.

Local first

Processing happens on your device by default. You decide what, if anything, ever leaves it.

Accessible to everyone

Accessibility features are built in and never placed behind a paywall.

Transparent

We tell you plainly what the software does and what data it touches, and our legal documents are written in plain language.

Our Products

Two products built on the same principles: Ethervox Workspace for your documents on the Mac, and Friend'O'Mine for everyday conversation on Android.
